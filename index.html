Every event announced on UoB society Instagram accounts in the last seven days, pulled from public posts and sorted by day. Room details are as written by each society; check their post before you go.

This is an unofficial student project, not run by the University of Birmingham or the Guild of Students. Locations marked "Not specified" usually appear on the poster image or in the society's WhatsApp group.
