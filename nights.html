Student nights

Nights out for UoB students, in one place.

Club nights and student nights in Selly Oak and across Birmingham.

Club nights are run by clubs and promoters; society nights are run by UoB societies and some are for members only, so check the society's post. Most are 18+: bring photo ID, and student ID where it says students only. Prices are from the ticket page and can change.

whatson is an unofficial student project. It is not run by the University of Birmingham or the Guild of Students. Check the ticket page before you go.
