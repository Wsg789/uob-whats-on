About whatson

Every university has hundreds of student societies, and almost all of them announce events on their own Instagram. Unless you follow every one, you miss most of what is happening. whatson puts those events on one page, sorted by day, with the time and the room.

Where it started

whatson began at the University of Birmingham as one page listing every society event on campus for the week ahead, so students could find things to do beyond the societies they already follow, and societies could get their events in front of people who had never heard of them.

The same problem exists at every university, so we now run whatson for other universities too, to bring each one’s society events together in one place. Pick your university from the list at the top of the events page.

How accurate is it?

The details are only as good as the post they came from, and plans change. Rooms move, events sell out and things get cancelled after the list is built. Always check the society’s own page before you go; every listing links to it.

For societies

Listings are free and you do not need to sign up for anything.

  • To be listed: put the date, time and room in the caption of your event post. Details that only appear on the poster image are easy to miss.
  • To fix a listing: message us with the correct details and we will update it as soon as we can.
  • To remove a listing, or stop being listed at all: message us and we will take it down. You do not need to give a reason.

Contact: message the account for your university on Instagram: @uobwhatson (Birmingham), @astonwhatson (Aston), @bristolwhatson (Bristol), @edinburghwhatson (Edinburgh), @kclwhatson (King’s), @manchesterwhatson (Manchester), @nottinghamwhatson (Nottingham), @qmulwhatson (Queen Mary), @uclwhatson (UCL) or @warwickwhatson (Warwick).

Privacy

whatson has no accounts, no adverts and no cookies. Visits are counted anonymously with GoatCounter, which records the page, the site you came from and your screen size, and stores nothing that identifies you. The only thing saved on your device is your filter preference. The pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when a page loads.
